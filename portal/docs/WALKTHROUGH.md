# Five-minute walkthrough for the committee

Run this from one laptop, on a projector or a shared screen. Start from **Settings → Reset demo** so the sample data is fresh. Switch roles with the **Demo** menu in the top bar.

## 1. Student: submit a semester (1 minute)

1. Sign in as **Student** (Tendai Moyo).
2. Home shows the status card, the open Autumn 2026/27 window, expiring documents, and the election banner.
3. Open **Semesters → Autumn 2026/27 session**. Choose the outcome, add one arrears subject, drop any PDF or image, and submit.
4. Point out the timeline at the bottom: every step is dated.

## 2. Verifier: query the result (1 minute)

1. Switch to **Verifier**. The queue badge shows Tendai's new submission.
2. Open it. The file sits beside the profile summary, with the five-point checklist.
3. Press **Q** (or the Query button), write "The dean's stamp is missing on page 2", and send.

## 3. Student: reply, verifier approves (1 minute)

1. Switch to **Student**. The notice bell shows the query. Open it and reply with a new file.
2. Switch to **Verifier**, open the submission again, tick all five points, and press **V**.
3. Switch back to **Student**: the semester is verified, and the notice says so.

## 4. Student: appeal a rejected result (30 seconds)

1. Still as **Student**, open **Spring 2025/26 session**, which was rejected.
2. Press **Appeal this decision**, give a reason, and send. The deadline is 14 days from the decision.
3. Switch to **Appeals officer** to show the appeal waiting. The original decision cannot be edited from here.

## 5. Executive: post a notice with a poster (30 seconds)

1. Switch to **Executive**. Home shows statistics by status, city, and university.
2. Open **Announcements**, add a title, a poster image, and its alt text, choose "Kazan only", and publish.
3. Open **Audit log** to show who did what. Ballots never appear in it.

## 6. Student votes, officer publishes (1 minute)

1. Switch to **Student** and open **Elections → Election portal**. Press **Start voting**, choose one candidate per office (or abstain), check the review screen, and cast.
2. The receipt shows only the time and a code. "Your choices are not shown here, by design."
3. Switch to **Election officer**. Turnout went up by one. Totals stay sealed while voting is open.
4. Press **Move to closed**, check the totals with the scrutineer, then **Move to published**, and **Put winners on committee cards**.
5. Switch to **Student** and open **Elections → Current committee** to show the new cards.

## If there is time

- **I need help now** (the red button) → slide to send. Switch to **Welfare** to see the alert with next of kin and a call button.
- **Coordinator** → Moscow students, the guide editor, and mentor pairing.
- Any role → open a page it should not see, for example `/admin/audit` as Student, to show the rule that blocks it.
