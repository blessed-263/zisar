## Consent wording

**For committee approval.** Shown on first sign-in and in **Profile → Consent**. Version 1.0.

1. ZISAR keeps your profile, your documents, and your verified semester results so the association can support you during your studies in Russia.
2. Inside ZISAR, only named verifiers can open your documents, and every opening is logged. Coordinators see your city details but not your medical notes or help requests.
3. ZISAR can give the Presidential and National Scholarships Department a report on your semester status and missing documents. That report contains no passport images unless a named officer produces a special export, and that action is logged.
4. You can download your own profile and documents at any time.

Button: **I agree**. The date and version are stored with the profile. A new version asks every student again.

## Elections overview: how elections work

**For committee approval.** Shown on **Elections → Overview**. The constitution is the final word; this text must match it.

1. Every enrolled member with a complete identity and studies section can vote. Members on suspension cannot.
2. A member can stand for one office in each election. A nomination needs a proposer and a seconder, who each confirm it in the portal.
3. The election officer checks each nomination against the constitution and gives a reason for any that is declined.
4. City representatives are elected only by members studying in that city.
5. Voting is secret. The portal records that you voted, and separately adds your choice to the totals. The two are never linked, and nobody can see who voted for whom.
6. The result stands if turnout reaches the quorum. A tie is settled as the constitution says, not by the portal.

Questions for the committee:

- What is the quorum? The prototype uses 40%.
- Who appoints the election officer and the scrutineer, and can either be a candidate? The prototype says no.
- How is a tie settled?

## Sample university guide

**For committee approval.** A model for coordinators. Shown under **University guides**. Replace the details with the real ones for each university.

### Registration

Within 7 working days of arriving, take your passport, migration card, and visa to the international office (main building, room 114, Monday to Friday, 10:00 to 16:00). They register your address. Keep the tear-off slip with your passport; police can ask for it.

### Hostel

Pay the hostel fee at the hostel office, not in cash to anyone else. Ask for a receipt every time. If something in your room is broken, report it in writing to the commandant, and keep a photo.

### Dean's office

Your result sheet (ведомость) for each session comes from the dean's office of your faculty. It must carry the dean's stamp. Ask for it in the week after your last exam; it can take several days.

### Medical

Your insurance policy lists a clinic. Go there first, with your passport and policy. In an emergency, call 112.

### Who to call

Your city coordinator, and the ZISAR welfare officers through **Help** in the portal. A senior student from your university can be your first call; ask your coordinator for a mentor.
